Power BI dashboard · BUas, year 1 · 2026

Is the Netherlands on track?

A five-page Power BI dashboard for SDG 13 (Climate Action). It shows how Dutch CO₂ emissions per person developed from 1990 to 2024 compared with the rest of the EU-27, and where they are heading by 2030.

# findingThe Netherlands cut CO₂ per person by 42% since 1990, the same pace as the EU. But it started late and is still the 7th highest emitter of 27.

  • Cleaned 26,509 rows of Our World in Data figures down to 980 with repeatable Power Query steps.
  • Wrote DAX measures for change since 1990 and EU rank, plus spread, correlation (r = −0.65) and a 2030 trend.
  • Kept one colour rule on every page: the Netherlands blue, the EU average black, other countries grey.

Presentation · Introduction to AI · 2026

When protection becomes control

A presentation that treats VIKI, the AI in I, Robot (2004), as a real system. It asks which AI abilities VIKI shows, whether today's warehouse robots could do the same, and who would carry the risk.

# conclusionUseful AI needs limits people can enforce: a clear task, a reliable way to stop it, and people who can question its decisions.

  • Mapped VIKI's abilities to four domains of the EU's AI Watch taxonomy.
  • Compared it with Amazon's DeepFleet robot fleet: partially feasible today.
  • Weighed safety, privacy and fairness risks for workers, citing 11 sources in IEEE style.
